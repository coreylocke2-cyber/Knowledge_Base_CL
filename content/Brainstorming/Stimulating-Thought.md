## Group Thinking

When stimulating thought, thinking in a group can help a great deal. When your stuck, bouncing your ideas off of another person can help you understand the ins and outs of that thought and whether you actually want to add that idea to your project or leave it as a thought. There is also the added benefit of more ideas; because the other person or people can interject and supply their own ideas to your topic, you could have an almost infinitely wider radius of choice.

Thinking in a group isn't always easy thought. A group of people is far harder to manage than a single person and this brings along issues that wouldn't have risen if you were alone.

Such as conflicts in the final product; Many people may want their ideas to be apart of the final product despite it not being agreed upon by the others in the group. When this arises, compromise could be come a big factor, or simply putting your foot down and declining the idea as a whole.

### Other Issues that Come with Group Thinking

- Unable to Meet at Proper Times
- Issues Arising Between Members
- Personal Issues
- Feeling Left out
- Lack of Participation
