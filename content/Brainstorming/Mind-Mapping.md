Mind Mapping is a visual technique that allows a person to visualize their task in a way that allows connects either between individual task, connecting to one larger task, or a combination of the two.

To Start you place the main topic in the middle of what ever program your working with, paper and pencil would work just as well. And from there you continue to add connecting task, aspects and whatever else would be connected to that main task, but the important thing is to not over explain what that connected branch does. 

Mind Mapping is for creating a framework for the mind, a way to quickly connect different things for organization, if it were to become too complicated you could quickly loose track of information or even become jumbled in what does what.

From that point, continue adding smaller branches that connect to the main ones if the task calls for it, this creates a deeper sense of organization that can help maintain a more intensive flow.
