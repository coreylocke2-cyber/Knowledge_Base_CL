
When brainstorming it's important to take time to think. What I mean by this is, don't force the thought to the forefront of your mind the entire time. It's important to focus on the task at hand but spending to much time thinking on the same topic can develop a sort of tunnel vision that causes frustration when you try to think about the topic. 

### Tunnel Vision

Tunnel vision is when the mind is unable to think about anything else, or when the viewpoint that is being made is the only thing that's within focus. At first this may seem like a positive thing, being able to focus on a task is something that most people tend to struggle with without effort, but focusing too much on a task can make it difficult to think about anything else that project has to offer.

Tunnel vision takes up a person view, and makes it difficult to think about other parts of your project, such as peroration or another part of it that should've been addressed.
