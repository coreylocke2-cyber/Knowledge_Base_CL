
Note-Taking is the practice of recording from a source such as oral presentation, web platform, or any other number of possible sources; but Note-Taking alone isn't something that can only be used when aquiring new information from differing sources, it also helps with brainstorming.

When Brainstorming, it's almost imperative that notes on the conversation are being made so that ideas are being organized and gathered in an easily accessible space that allows for changes and for more information to be added or expounded upon.

### How to Take Notes

Many think that Taking Notes is simply writing down what the teacher says or what's being said in a book, but this couldn't be further from the truth. When taking notes, you need to rewrite what is being said in a way that you can understand, that is quick, and isn't complicated. 

Firstly, an example of this would be taking the words "When believing in something, you are already part way to making that something into reality" the way this could be transferred into a notes like format would be "Believing is half the battle."

Next, being quick when taking notes is something that almost all students inspire to do because they don't want to take time away from the class and hinder teaching when there is still so much more to be taught. This also applies to the work setting, when a person is giving a presentation and the last thing you would want to do is interrupt the workflow because of a question you had during the last slide.
