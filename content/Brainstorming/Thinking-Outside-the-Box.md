
Thinking outside of the box is another way of saying 'think without limiting yourself'. More people limit the way they think by only focusing on the task a hand and not the tasks that surround it. In the term 'Thinking-Outside-the-Box' the box would of course be the project or task that you had been given, assigned, or came up with and the outside of the box would be the way that the task is approached, what the outcome of the task can be; essentially how the project could effect or be effected the future and or the present.


## Flipping your Thought Process

Initially a person may think that flipping your thought process would be a way of thinking outside of the box for the same issue that is being addressed. While it is a form of thinking out side of the box, it focuses on a completely different aspect which is the issues that could arise with the project. 

For example you're making a website for some sort of shopping company; How would a person access a specific part of the website, is there an easily accessible help line, how can a person find what they're looking for without having to dig through your entire page?
